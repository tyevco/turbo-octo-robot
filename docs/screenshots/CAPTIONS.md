# Screenshot captions

Made by `node --import ./tools/node/register.mjs tools/shots/hero.mjs [--only name,...]`. Every image is a real render of the shipped mod through the screenshot rig, and every shot comes from the same save: the second run after the first wish, with 10 Wishes and a herd of 34. string.png shows that game later on, and away.png shows it seven hours on. The close-ups are crops of whole-window renders. themes.png and jump.png place such crops side by side under plain labels.

**What is real and what is drawn.** The game panel and the ribbon are the shipped Creaseworks mod, rendered by the rig. herdr's own chrome (the sidebar, tabs, pane headers, the terminal transcripts and the status bar) is drawn by the rig's mock window (`tools/render/window.mjs`, with per-pane transcripts in `tools/shots/hero.mjs`). It is not a screenshot of herdr. The rig draws a "+N" pop at its click point before the pop's 0.7 s rise. Hand hits are at least 80 ms apart, so one frame holds one pop at full strength: the last click's.

**Sizes.** The whole-window images are 1600 x 1000 CSS px, shot at 2x (3200 x 2000). The game panel is dragged to 467 px wide. String and away use the mod's standard 320 px panel, because the strand card and the away card are at most 304 px wide and fill it. The rail is shot in a 1280 x 1000 window.

**README use.** hero-dark.png is the top image. hero-light.png is not a second gallery image. It is the `prefers-color-scheme: light` source of the same `<picture>` (`<source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="docs/screenshots/hero-light.png">` with hero-dark.png as the `<img>`). The cutting mat stays green in every theme, so the two differ only in herdr's chrome and the panel's surfaces.
